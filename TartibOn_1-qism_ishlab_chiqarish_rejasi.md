## 3. 1-QISM — Piyodalar o‘tish joylari

**Qoida nomi:** Tartibga solinmagan piyodalar o‘tish joyida piyodalarga yo‘l berish
**Manba:** YHQ, 17-bob (bandlar raqami LexUZ’da tekshirilishi kerak)
**Tekshiruv holati:** ⚠️ Mavzu mosligi ikkilamchi manbadan olingan. Diktor matnining huquqiy mazmuni LexUZ bilan solishtirilishi shart.

### 3.1 Video promptlar (ingliz tili, har kadr 5–8 s)

Doimiy uslub qatori (har promptga qo‘shiladi):
`Photorealistic 3D render, white Chevrolet Malibu 2 sedan with carbon hood, red brake calipers, rear wing, "TartibOn" logo on front doors (blue "Tartib", green "On" with power icon), Uzbek city setting, Tashkent-style wide avenues, Latin-script Uzbek signage, cinematic lighting, 16:9, no text overlays, no voice.`

**1.1 — Kirish (6 s)**
`Aerial drone shot slowly descending over a Tashkent avenue in morning light; the white TartibOn Malibu drives in the right lane at moderate city speed; plane trees, modern buildings, light traffic.`

**1.2 — Belgiga yaqinlashish (6 s)**
`Driver's-eye view from inside the Malibu; ahead, a blue square "pedestrian crossing" sign with white triangle and walking figure (European/CIS Vienna-convention style), white zebra stripes on asphalt 60 m ahead; dashboard visible, speedometer needle steady.`
> ⚠️ Belgi ko‘pincha noto‘g‘ri chiqadi. Montajda rasmiy belgi tasvirini (5.19.1/5.19.2 — raqamni LexUZ ilovasidan tekshiring) ustiga qo‘ying.

**1.3 — Sekinlashish (5 s)**
`Side tracking shot: the Malibu's brake lights glow softly, car smoothly decelerates as it nears the zebra crossing; a young woman with a backpack steps onto the crossing from the right sidewalk.`

**1.4 — To‘g‘ri harakat: to‘xtash (6 s)**
`Front low-angle shot: the Malibu stopped fully before the stop line, wheels still; the pedestrian crosses calmly in front of the car and reaches the far sidewalk; an elderly man with a cane follows.`

**1.5 — Davom etish (5 s)**
`Rear three-quarter shot: crossing now empty, the Malibu pulls away smoothly, TartibOn logo visible on the door.`

**1.6 — Noto‘g‘ri harakat, xavfsiz simulyatsiya (7 s)**
`Split-screen-style educational scene: a translucent grey "ghost" version of a generic sedan drives through the zebra crossing without slowing while a pedestrian is crossing; the scene freezes before any contact, a red outline highlights the danger zone; no impact, no injury shown.`

**1.7 — Qo‘shni yo‘lakdagi mashina to‘xtagan holat (8 s)**
`Two-lane road, a white delivery van stops before a zebra crossing in the right lane, blocking the view; the Malibu in the left lane slows down and stops beside it; a child emerges from behind the van onto the crossing; the Malibu stays stopped until the child passes.`

**1.8 — Avtobus bekati (7 s)**
`City bus stopped at a bus stop with passengers boarding; the Malibu approaches from behind in the right lane, slows, gives the bus space as the bus signals left to depart.`

**1.9 — Yakun (5 s)**
`Golden-hour wide shot: the Malibu drives away down the avenue, the zebra crossing in foreground with pedestrians walking safely.`

### 3.2 Diktor matnlari (ElevenLabs uchun, o‘zbek lotin)

> ⚠️ Huquqiy mazmun LexUZ bilan tekshirilmagan. Ovoz yozishdan oldin 17-bob bandlari bilan solishtiring.

**1.1** — Assalomu alaykum! TartibOn o‘quv serialiga xush kelibsiz. Bugun piyodalar o‘tish joylari haqida gaplashamiz.

**1.2** — Ko‘k fonli piyodalar o‘tish joyi belgisi va oq chiziqlar — oldinda piyodalar yo‘lni kesib o‘tishi mumkinligini bildiradi. Bu yerda svetofor yo‘q, demak bu tartibga solinmagan o‘tish joyi.

**1.3** — Belgini oldindan ko‘ring. Tezlikni oldindan pasaytiring, shunda kerak bo‘lsa, xavfsiz to‘xtay olasiz.

**1.4** — Piyoda o‘tish joyiga chiqdi. Haydovchi to‘xtadi va unga yo‘l berdi. Keksalar va bolalar sekinroq yurishini unutmang — to‘liq o‘tib bo‘lishini kuting.

**1.5** — O‘tish joyi bo‘shadi. Endi harakatni xotirjam davom ettirish mumkin.

**1.6** — Bu noto‘g‘ri harakat. Piyodaga yo‘l bermasdan o‘tib ketish og‘ir oqibatlarga olib kelishi mumkin. Bunday qilmang.

**1.7** — Diqqat! Qo‘shni yo‘lakda mashina o‘tish joyi oldida to‘xtagan. Uning orqasida piyoda bo‘lishi mumkin. Siz ham sekinlang va to‘xtang.

**1.8** — Avtobus bekatida ehtiyot bo‘ling: yo‘lovchilar avtobus oldidan yoki orqasidan chiqib qolishi mumkin.

**1.9** — Piyodaga yo‘l berish — bu hurmat va xavfsizlik. Keyingi qismda ko‘rishguncha!
